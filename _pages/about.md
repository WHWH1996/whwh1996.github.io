I am currently working at Huawei Hong Kong Research Center, focusing on Networking & AI infrastructure. I obtained my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the [Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST)](https://www.ust.hk/) in 2025, where I was advised by [Prof. Kai Chen](http://www.cse.ust.hk/~kaichen/) at the [intelligent System and networkING Lab (iSING Lab)](https://ising.cse.ust.hk/). Prior to my doctoral studies, I received my Bachelor of Science in Computer Science from [Peking University](https://www.pku.edu.cn/) in 2019 as an Outstanding Graduate.

My research interests include distributed machine learning system, data center networking, programmable switch and large language models.
